## A practice rooted in Siliguri

The home base of Yoga with Komal is Siliguri, North Bengal. Group classes meet in person here, with online options for anyone outside the city or away that week.

## What's offered in Siliguri

- **Group Hatha-Vinyasa classes**, 90 minutes, twice weekly.
- **1:1 sessions**, online or in person, ₹1,000/session.
- **Themed sessions** — Yoga Nidra, devotional chanting, full and new moon gatherings.

## Who's already practicing here

Students from Siliguri, Bagdogra, Jalpaiguri, Kurseong, and across the broader North Bengal and Sikkim regions. Some travel in for offline classes; others practice live online from the hills.

## How to start

The exact venue and timing for offline sessions go out with your booking confirmation. Tap "Join via WhatsApp" to reserve a place.
