## A women-led, women-friendly practice

Komal teaches a slow, embodied yoga practice in Siliguri, online and in person. The space is small and intentional. The work is held with women in mind, though everyone is welcome.

## What "women-friendly" means here

- **No posing, no proving.** The focus is on how you feel, not how it looks.
- **A practice that meets the cycle.** Slow Flow when you have energy, Yin & Breath when you don't, Yoga Nidra for the days the world is too much.
- **A teacher who's lived in this body.** Komal teaches from her own practice, including the parts of the cycle, sleep, anxiety, and rest that don't come up in conventional classes.
- **Small groups.** No giant studio, no awkward back row.

## What students come for

- Recovering after illness, surgery, or burnout.
- Returning to movement after pregnancy or postpartum.
- Settling an anxious or wired nervous system.
- Building a steady practice that doesn't feel performative.
- Preparing for a steadier next chapter, of work, of motherhood, of midlife.
