## A practice built around you

Group classes are wonderful. They're also, by nature, the average. A 1:1 session is the opposite: a practice shaped entirely by what your body, your week, and your life are asking for right now.

## What a 1:1 looks like

We start with a short conversation. Sometimes that's all about a recent injury. Sometimes it's about sleep, anxiety, or a wedding next month. Sometimes it's that you've been doing yoga for a decade and something has stopped landing.

A typical session might include:

- **Movement** to release tension and rebuild range.
- **Breath awareness** and guided pranayama.
- **Chanting** to create stillness when the mind is loud.
- **Reflection on the Yoga Sutras**, especially the Samadhi Pada, when there's space for it.
- **Journaling prompts** to integrate.
