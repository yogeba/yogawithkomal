## Live, recorded, joinable from anywhere

Online classes with Komal run live and are recorded for replay the same day. You join from wherever you are, India, the UK, the US, the foothills of Sikkim, and you have access to the recording for two weeks.

## What's offered online

- **Group Hatha-Vinyasa classes**, 90 minutes.
- **Yoga Nidra** sessions, 45 minutes of guided rest.
- **1:1 sessions**, scheduled to fit your week.
- **New Moon Circle**, monthly.

## What you need

- A mat (or a thick rug, fine to start).
- A quiet corner with enough space to lie down.
- A wall for some shapes.
- A couple of household props (a cushion, a thick book, a belt). I'll always offer alternatives.
